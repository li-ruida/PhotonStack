## v22 large corpus

The 500-image v22 corpus is registered machine-readably in
`tests/artifact-blindset/corpus-v22.json` rather than duplicating 500 rows in
this table. Each manifest entry records the Commons source page, fixed download
URL, author/credit metadata, license name and URL, original dimensions,
Commons SHA-1, and the cached rendition's SHA-256.

The source-category composition is 48 Satellite trails, 17 Satellite flares,
35 Aircraft light trails, 49 Meteor trails, 57 Meteors, 44 Meteor showers, 82
Star trails, and 168 Circumpolar stars in star trails. These are Commons
category-derived weak labels and are not represented as individual human or
pixel-level annotations. Image bytes remain in the ignored
`build/artifact-corpus/v22-special-1280/` cache.

Both v22 splits have AI visual-triage layers in
`tests/artifact-blindset/corpus-v22-ai-review.json` and its `-development`
counterpart. They retain source attribution from the v22 manifest. The
resulting 349 high-confidence cases are regression/development data, not human
ground truth; 151 medium/low-confidence or unsuitable cases remain queued for
human review.

User-provided Desktop photographs are evaluated in place only. They are not
copied into the repository or used for model training without explicit
permission.
